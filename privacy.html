Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who We Are

White Cloth Cleaning ("we", "us", "our") is a professional cleaning service operating throughout Hertfordshire. Our contact email is hello@whiteclothcleaning.co.uk.

2. What Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request or contact us, we collect:

  • Your name and contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Your address or postcode
  • Details about the service you require
  • Any additional information you choose to provide in your message

We do not collect payment information directly — any payments are handled by secure third-party processors.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • Schedule and carry out cleaning services you have booked
  • Send you service confirmations and follow-up communications
  • Improve the quality of our service

We will never sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the basis of your consent (when you submit an enquiry form) and for the performance of a contract (when you book a cleaning service).

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so promptly unless we are legally required to retain it.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at hello@whiteclothcleaning.co.uk.

7. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies required for the site to function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at hello@whiteclothcleaning.co.uk.
