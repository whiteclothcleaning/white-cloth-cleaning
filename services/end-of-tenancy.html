What's Included

Every end of tenancy cleaning carried out by White Cloth in Hertfordshire includes the following as standard.

  • Full property deep clean to inventory standard
  • Oven, grill, hob and extractor fan clean
  • Fridge, freezer and all white goods
  • Inside cupboards & wardrobes cleaned
  • Tile scrubbing & descaling
  • Bathroom: full descale, grout scrub, sanitisation
  • Limescale removal from all fixtures and fittings
  • Skirting boards, doors & frames cleaned
  • Full property dusting & vacuuming
  • Deep floor cleaning & mopping

  • Inventory checklist provided on completion

FAQs

How much does end of tenancy cleaning cost in Hertfordshire?
End of tenancy cleaning is priced based on the size of the property. We provide free, fixed quotes — contact us with the number of bedrooms and bathrooms and we'll come back to you within 60 minutes with a price.
Will I get my deposit back?
Our end of tenancy clean is specifically designed to meet landlord and agency inventory standards. We clean every area of the property methodically to give you the best possible chance of getting your full deposit back.
How much notice do you need for an end of tenancy clean?
We recommend booking as early as possible, ideally at least 5–7 days before your move-out date. For urgent bookings, contact us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate.
Do I need to be present during the end of tenancy clean?
You don't need to be present. Most tenants provide access and we carry out the clean while they complete their move. We can hand keys back to you, your landlord or letting agent on completion.
