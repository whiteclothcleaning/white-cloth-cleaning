FAQs

How long does a deep clean take?
A deep clean typically takes 4–8 hours depending on the size and condition of the property. We'll give you a time estimate when we provide your free quote.
How much does a deep clean cost in Hertfordshire?
Deep clean pricing depends on the size of your property and specific requirements. We provide a free, fixed quote after discussing your needs — no surprises on the day.
How often should I book a deep clean?
Most homeowners book a deep clean once or twice a year, in addition to their regular domestic cleaning. It's also popular when moving into a new property, before hosting events, or after building work.
Do you deep clean rental properties?
Yes. Deep cleaning rental properties across Hertfordshire is a significant part of our work. We clean to the standard required by landlords and letting agencies, and our end of tenancy service includes a 48-hour re-clean guarantee.
