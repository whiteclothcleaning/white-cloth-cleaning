  • Full vacuuming of all carpets and hard floors

  • Bathroom and toilet sanitisation

FAQs

How much does domestic cleaning cost in Hertfordshire?
Our domestic cleaning service starts from £20 per hour across all Hertfordshire areas including Watford, St Albans, Hemel Hempstead, Berkhamsted, Radlett, Harpenden and Chorleywood. There are no hidden fees — the hourly rate is all you pay.
How often should I book a domestic clean?
Most clients book weekly or fortnightly, though we also offer monthly cleans. The right frequency depends on the size of your property, number of occupants and personal preference. We're happy to advise during your free quote.
Will I get the same cleaner each time?
We always aim to send the same cleaner to each client, so they get to know your home and preferences. If your regular cleaner is unavailable, we'll always notify you in advance.
Do I need to be home during the clean?
Completely up to you. Many of our Hertfordshire clients provide a key and go about their day. Our cleaners are all DBS checked and fully insured, so you can trust them to let themselves in.
